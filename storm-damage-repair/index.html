Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season

Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Poway

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
